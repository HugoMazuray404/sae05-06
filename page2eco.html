Ankama Ecology

main guidelines and ecological actions



Energy Consumption

maintenance

Ankama's servers and digital infrastructure consume electricity to run their online games. This consumption contributes to the company's ecological footprint.

Carbon Footprint

Ankama's digital activities produce CO₂ emissions related to servers and IT equipment. Business travel can also increase these emissions.

Physical Products

Manga, figurines, and cards require paper, plastic, and other raw materials. Their manufacturing and transport have an impact on the environment.

Human & Technology



Commuting & Transport

Employees can use different modes of transport to get to work or go on business trips. Product shipping to customers also generates CO₂ emissions.

Impact Reduction

Ankama can limit its ecological impact by reducing printing and packaging. Using more energy-efficient equipment and managing resources better can also help.

Server Maintenance

Partnership with OVHcloud for server cooling systems that mainly rely on liquid cooling technology.
