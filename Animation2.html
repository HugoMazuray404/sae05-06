Our Animations

Wakfu

Wakfu is a French animated series created by Anthony Roux. Launched in 2008, it is set in the universe of the video game Dofus.

Synopsis: The story follows Yugo, a young boy with mysterious powers, who sets out in search of his origins and biological family. Accompanied by his friends, he travels across the World of Twelve and faces many dangers.

Dofus: Book 1 – Julith

Dofus: Book 1 – Julith is a French animated movie directed by Anthony Roux and Jean-Jacques Denis. It was released in 2016.

Synopsis: The story follows Joris, a young boy living in Bonta with his adoptive father, Kerubim Crepin. When the witch Julith returns seeking revenge, Joris must protect Bonta and its inhabitants.

Mutafukaz

Mutafukaz is a French-Japanese animated film directed by Guillaume "Run" Renard and Shojiro Nishimi. It was released in 2018.

Synopsis: The story follows Angelino, a young man living in Dark Meat City. After a scooter accident, he begins experiencing disturbing visions and discovers he is being hunted by mysterious agents.

Princess Dragon

Princess Dragon is a French animated film directed by Jean-Jacques Denis and Anthony Roux. It was released in 2021.

Synopsis: Bristle, a young girl raised by a dragoness, discovers the world of humans and must face many dangers to protect her family.

Abraca

Abraca is a French animated series created by Ankama. It is inspired by fairy tales and magic.

Synopsis: Abraca travels through a world filled with magical creatures, wizards, and characters inspired by traditional fairy tales.

Dofus: Kerub's Bazaar

Dofus: Kerub's Bazaar is a French animated series created by Ankama and broadcast starting in 2013.

Synopsis: Kerubim Crepin, a retired adventurer turned shopkeeper, tells Joris about the extraordinary adventures he experienced in the past.
